FAQ

Before you book

What happens at the first appointment?

A 30-minute consultation with skin imaging and a review of your medical history and current products. You leave with a written plan and costs. It’s free, and there is no obligation to book treatment.

How much downtime should I plan for?

None for the Hydrafacial or LED. Expect one to two days of pinkness after a chemical peel or RF microneedling, and three to five days of redness after fractional laser. We schedule around events if you tell us the date.

Can I be treated if I’m pregnant or breastfeeding?

We offer Hydrafacials and LED during pregnancy. Peels, microneedling and laser are postponed until after breastfeeding. Your clinician will confirm at consultation.

Are your treatments safe for darker skin tones?

Yes. Our devices and peel strengths are chosen to be suitable across Fitzpatrick types I–VI, and we patch-test before any resurfacing treatment on deeper skin tones.

What is your cancellation policy?

Rescheduling or cancelling is free with 48 hours’ notice. Later changes are charged at 50% of the treatment price, waived for illness.
